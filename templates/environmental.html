Environmental Studies

The Environmental Studies program focuses on understanding environmental issues and developing sustainable solutions to address challenges facing our planet.

Program Overview

  • Study environmental science and policy.
  • Gain practical experience through fieldwork and research.
  • Understand the impact of human activities on the environment.
  • Prepare for careers in environmental advocacy, research, and management.

Career Prospects

  • Environmental health and safety managers develop and implement programs to ensure compliance with environmental regulations and promote workplace safety.
  • Wildlife biologists study animals and their habitats, working to develop conservation plans and strategies to protect endangered species.
  • Environmental policy analysts assess and develop policies that promote environmental sustainability and protect natural resources, often working with governments, NGOs, and private organizations.
  • Environmental educators teach and promote awareness about environmental issues and sustainable practices in schools, communities, and organizations, fostering a deeper understanding and commitment to environmental stewardship.
  • Environmental scientists conduct research to identify, control, or eliminate sources of pollutants or hazards affecting the environment or public health.
  • Conservation biologists work to protect and preserve biodiversity by studying ecosystems and species, developing conservation plans, and implementing strategies to protect natural habitats.
