Business Administration

The Business Administration program offers a comprehensive education in various aspects of business including finance, marketing, and management. Students will gain a solid foundation in business principles and practices, preparing them for a dynamic and evolving business world.

Program Overview

  • Learn the fundamental concepts of management and leadership.
  • Gain an understanding of financial statements and accounting principles.
  • Explore the principles and practices of marketing.
  • Study the ethical issues and challenges in business.

Career Prospects

  • Business Analysts: Assess and improve business processes.
  • Marketing Managers: Develop and execute strategies to promote products and services.
  • Financial Advisors: Provide expert guidance on managing finances and investments.
  • Human Resources Managers: Oversee employee relations and organizational development.
  • Operations Managers: Ensure the efficient production and delivery of goods and services.
  • Consultants: Offer strategic advice to help businesses grow.
  • Entrepreneurs: Start and manage their own businesses.
  • Sales Managers: Drive sales and manage sales teams.
  • Product Managers and Project Managers: Oversee product development and execution.
  • Management Consultants: Enhance business performance.
  • Account Managers: Handle client relationships.
  • Brand Managers: Build and maintain a company's brand identity.
