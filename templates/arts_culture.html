Arts & Culture

Immerse yourself in the vibrant arts and culture scene on our campus. From galleries to theaters, we offer a variety of venues and programs to nurture creativity and celebrate artistic expression.

Facilities

Our arts and culture facilities cover an area of over 150,000 square feet and include:

  • Art Gallery:
    • Area: 20,000 sq ft
    • Features: Exhibition spaces, student showcases, visiting artist displays
  • Performing Arts Theater:
    • Area: 30,000 sq ft
    • Features: Stage, seating for 500, advanced lighting and sound systems
  • Music Hall:
    • Area: 25,000 sq ft
    • Features: Concert stage, practice rooms, recording studio
  • Dance Studio:
    • Area: 15,000 sq ft
    • Features: Sprung floors, mirrors, barre
  • Creative Writing Center:
    • Area: 10,000 sq ft
    • Features: Workshop rooms, literary readings, guest lectures
  • Film and Media Center:
    • Area: 25,000 sq ft
    • Features: Screening room, editing suites, production equipment
Programs and Events

We offer a wide range of programs and events to engage the campus community in arts and culture:

  • Art exhibitions and gallery talks
  • Theater productions and performances
  • Music concerts and recitals
  • Dance performances and workshops
  • Creative writing workshops and readings
  • Film screenings and festivals
