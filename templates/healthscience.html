Health Sciences

The Health Sciences program provides comprehensive education in various aspects of health and medicine, preparing students for careers in healthcare and medical research.

Program Overview

  • Understand human anatomy and physiology.
  • Gain practical skills through clinical experience.
  • Study health promotion and disease prevention.
  • Prepare for careers in healthcare, research, and public health.

Career Prospects

  • Graduates of the Health Sciences program can look forward to a diverse range of rewarding career opportunities. As healthcare professionals, they provide critical medical care and support to patients, ensuring their health and well-being. Medical researchers play a pivotal role in advancing medical science by conducting groundbreaking studies, leading to the development of new treatments and technologies.
  • Public health specialists are essential in promoting community health through the development and implementation of health programs that prevent disease and improve quality of life.
  • Clinical coordinators oversee patient care and clinical operations, ensuring that healthcare services are delivered efficiently and effectively. These careers are highly fulfilling, offering the chance to make a significant impact in hospitals, research institutions, public health organizations, and clinical settings.
  • Health science is one of the most diverse areas of study in the United States right now, with students of all economic and educational backgrounds wanting to learn how to help others.
