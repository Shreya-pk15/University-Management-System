Campus Safety

Our comprehensive campus safety programs and facilities are designed to ensure the security and well-being of our students, faculty, and staff. We are committed to maintaining a safe and secure environment for everyone on campus.

Facilities and Services

Our campus safety facilities cover an area of over 50,000 square feet and include:

  • Campus Security Office:
    • Area: 15,000 sq ft
    • Services: 24/7 security patrol, emergency response
  • Emergency Call Stations:
    • Area: Distributed across campus
    • Features: Easily accessible call stations for immediate assistance
  • Surveillance Systems:
    • Area: Coverage across key areas on campus
    • Features: High-definition cameras, monitored 24/7
  • Health and Safety Office:
    • Area: 10,000 sq ft
    • Services: Health inspections, safety audits
Safety Programs

We offer a variety of programs to promote safety and security on campus:

  • Personal safety workshops
  • Self-defense classes
  • Emergency preparedness training
  • Fire safety drills
  • Crime prevention programs
