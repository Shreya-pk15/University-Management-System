Housing & Dining

Experience comfortable and convenient living on campus with our diverse housing options and dining facilities, offering a variety of cuisines to cater to different tastes and dietary needs.

Housing Options

We offer a range of housing options to meet the needs of our diverse student body. Our housing facilities cover a total area of over 500,000 square feet, providing ample space and comfort for all residents:

  • Traditional Dormitories:
    • Capacity: 2000 students
    • Area: 150,000 sq ft
    • Amenities: Shared bathrooms, common rooms, study areas
  • Suite-style Living:
    • Capacity: 1000 students
    • Area: 120,000 sq ft
    • Amenities: Private bathrooms, living areas, kitchenettes
  • Apartment-style Housing:
    • Capacity: 800 students
    • Area: 100,000 sq ft
    • Amenities: Full kitchens, private bedrooms, living rooms
  • Graduate Student Housing:
    • Capacity: 500 students
    • Area: 80,000 sq ft
    • Amenities: Single occupancy rooms, study areas, lounges
