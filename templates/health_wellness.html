Health & Wellness

Our comprehensive Health & Wellness facilities are designed to support the physical and mental well-being of our students. We offer a range of services to help you maintain a healthy lifestyle and cope with the demands of academic life.

Facilities

Our health and wellness facilities cover an area of over 100,000 square feet and include:

  • Student Health Center:
    • Area: 30,000 sq ft
    • Services: Medical consultations, immunizations, lab services
  • Counseling and Psychological Services:
    • Area: 20,000 sq ft
    • Services: Individual counseling, group therapy, crisis intervention
  • Wellness Center:
    • Area: 25,000 sq ft
    • Services: Fitness classes, stress management workshops, nutrition counseling
  • Recreation Center:
    • Area: 25,000 sq ft
    • Amenities: Indoor track, swimming pool, fitness equipment
Programs and Services

We offer a variety of programs and services to promote health and wellness on campus:

  • Health education workshops
  • Mental health awareness campaigns
  • Peer support groups
  • Fitness and wellness programs
  • Stress reduction and mindfulness sessions
